## Overview
An original review sample for the material inventory card, derived from the user's preference for restrained contemporary software surfaces. It replaces the rejected cream/green visual direction. It is not an approved library-wide style.

## Colors
Neutral graphite surfaces, near-white primary figures, legible secondary gray. No decorative accent stripe, glow, gradient text, or beige paper treatment. Depth comes from surface luminance and one restrained panel shadow.

## Typography
Use the macOS system sans-serif family with PingFang SC for Chinese. A large total is the first focal point; the two category totals are second. Small text is metadata only. Numerals use tabular figures and controlled tracking.

## Layout
1280 × 720 composition. One 1120 × 544 panel, inset 80/88. Header, left total, right category rows, and a bottom proportional track. The 24 videos and 36 images remain explicitly demo data; total 60 and 40/60 percent shares are arithmetic, not extra claims.

## Elevation & Depth
Use slight overlap only inside the illustrative media stack, where it communicates multiple assets. No extra background ornaments, ambient breathing or perpetual UI motion; restraint follows the user's explicit reference.

## Shapes
One continuous outer radius. Small rounded rectangles describe video frames and images. Icons are independently drawn SVG geometry.

## Components
The bottom track encodes 24:36. It is a data graphic, not an ornamental divider. Controls belong to preview.html and are absent from the composition.

## Do's and Don'ts
Preserve calm alignment and readable holds. No left-edge accent rules, gratuitous English labels, fake status badges, invented growth claims, or repetitive cards around every number.

Motion: surface settles first; total rises through a clipping window; each media stack opens before its category number; the proportional track resolves last. Hold the complete layout from 2.7 to 6 seconds. Do not fade to black.
